ROM from the SD card, mapper, save, RTC and peripherals from the inserted cartridge.
Insert the matching cartridge first. The startup screen compares the cartridge and ROM headers; if they differ it asks twice before the game can touch the cartridge save.
The save stays on the cartridge and no .sav is created. Back it up first.
Based on the openfpga-GBC port by budude2 and the MiSTer Gameboy core. Layered fork by TomJinW.
